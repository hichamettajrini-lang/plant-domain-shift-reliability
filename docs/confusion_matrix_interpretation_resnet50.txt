
The confusion matrix analysis confirmed the strong classification performance of the ResNet50 model on the PlantVillage validation subset. The model achieved an overall validation accuracy of 97.87%, with a weighted F1-score of 97.87%, indicating a high level of consistency between precision and recall across the 38 plant stress and disease classes.

Several classes were classified with perfect or near-perfect performance, including Apple Cedar apple rust, Blueberry healthy, Cherry healthy, Corn common rust, Corn healthy, Grape leaf blight, Grape healthy, Squash powdery mildew, Strawberry healthy, and several tomato virus-related classes. This suggests that ResNet50 was able to extract highly discriminative visual features when disease symptoms had clear and distinctive patterns.

However, the confusion matrix also revealed some remaining classification errors between visually similar disease categories. The most frequent confusion occurred between Corn Cercospora leaf spot / Gray leaf spot and Corn Northern Leaf Blight, with six samples misclassified. This is understandable because both diseases produce elongated or irregular leaf lesions with similar color and texture patterns. A second important confusion appeared between Tomato Late blight and Tomato Early blight, with five misclassified samples. These two diseases can show overlapping necrotic symptoms, especially when images contain advanced lesions or variable illumination.

Other notable confusions involved tomato diseases such as Target Spot, Spider mites, Septoria leaf spot, Leaf Mold, and Early blight. These errors indicate that most remaining mistakes occurred within the same crop species and between diseases presenting similar leaf discoloration, spots, or necrotic regions. Some cross-crop confusions were also observed, such as Potato Late blight being predicted as Tomato Late blight, which may reflect the visual similarity of blight symptoms across different plant species.

Overall, the confusion matrix shows that ResNet50 provides robust classification performance on controlled PlantVillage images. Nevertheless, the remaining errors highlight the difficulty of distinguishing diseases with similar visual symptoms. This supports the need for further external validation on more realistic datasets, such as PlantDoc or Plant Pathology 2020, to evaluate whether the model remains reliable under domain-shift conditions involving natural backgrounds, lighting variations, occlusions, and heterogeneous image acquisition settings.
