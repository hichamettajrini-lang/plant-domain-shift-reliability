
The comparative evaluation of the four transfer learning architectures showed clear differences in classification performance and computational cost. ResNet50 achieved the best validation performance, with a validation accuracy of 97.87% and the lowest validation loss of 0.0851. This indicates that ResNet50 was the most effective architecture for extracting discriminative visual features from the PlantVillage subset used in this experiment.

EfficientNetB0 obtained the second-best performance, with a validation accuracy of 96.19% and a validation loss of 0.1442. Although its accuracy was slightly lower than that of ResNet50, it required considerably less training time, making it a strong compromise between predictive performance and computational efficiency. DenseNet121 achieved a validation accuracy of 95.96%, but its training time was the highest among the tested models. MobileNetV2 produced the lowest validation accuracy, 95.01%, but it remained the fastest model, which makes it suitable for lightweight or embedded deployment scenarios.

Overall, ResNet50 provided the highest classification accuracy on the PlantVillage validation subset, while EfficientNetB0 offered the best balance between accuracy and training time. These findings suggest that the choice of architecture should depend on the intended application: ResNet50 is preferable when maximum accuracy is required, whereas EfficientNetB0 or MobileNetV2 may be more appropriate for real-time or resource-constrained plant stress monitoring systems.
